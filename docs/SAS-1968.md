## Primitive and compositional fitting

Taskledger task `task-0004` records the approved fitting redesign. V2 family fitting has been
rerun for the underturn, ascender-loop, hump, and open-oval primitives. Family checkpoints and
reports are stored under `data/training/sas-1968/`; private review sheets remain under
`work/sas-1968/overlays/`.

Corpus `active_instances` lists the topology instances that participate in each sample's
compositional explanation. Primitive-family fitting and glyph fitting use fit-split samples only.
Glyph fitting keeps shared primitive geometry fixed and writes occurrence-fit reports and
composition overlays under `work/sas-1968/glyph-fits/`. Validation and hold-out samples do not
select or update parameters.

The SAS `fit-config.yaml` selects objective version 2 and the alternating least-squares optimizer.
Geometric, prior, and family-balance weights are configured there. Each entry in `families.yaml`
can constrain its occurrence-alignment parameter set and bounds; these deltas do not change shared
primitive geometry during glyph fitting.

Run glyph fitting and word diagnostics from the repository root:

```sh
inkgrammar fit fit-glyphs data/training/sas-1968/project.yaml
inkgrammar fit validate-words data/training/sas-1968/project.yaml
```

Word validation is diagnostic only. Word samples identify active instances as
`character-index:instance-id`. The validator composes fitted glyph occurrences at their declared
advance widths, reports within-glyph attachment gaps, and marks glyph-boundary connections as
unspecified when no contextual rule is present. It does not optimize parameters. The current SAS
fitting corpus contains no word samples, so `validate-words` produces no reports for SAS until
reviewed word observations are added. Private source scans and materializations must remain
outside the repository.

## Source ingestion

The five prepared PNGs were verified against their supplied SHA-256 hashes and
dimensions. Source records are in `data/observations/sas-1968/sources.json`;
source hashes, dimensions and observation coordinates are in
`data/observations/sas-1968/manifest.json`.


Reproduce ingestion from the supplied bundle, from the repository root:

```sh
inkgrammar page extract \
  work/sas-1968/sources/sas-1968-01-alphabet-plate.png \
  --layout examples/layouts/sas-1968-alphabet.yaml \
  --output work/sas-1968/observations/alphabet
python -m tools.ingest_sas_sources
```

The 63 label assignments were visually inspected in the extraction review:
uppercase A through Z, lowercase a through z plus ß (in the plate's order),
and digits 1 through 9 followed by 0. Wide/overlapping capitals, descending
lowercase forms and ß were checked. Label acceptance is not fitting acceptance.
The rectangular extraction regions can contain neighboring ink or intersect
strokes, particularly around O/P/Q and o/p/q. Those samples are explicitly marked
`needs-isolation`; other samples still require fitting review. Gap confidence
is not used to reject a correct label.

Observation IDs are Unicode-based and independent of extraction ordering.
Crop locators are `local-only:` references, not runtime dependencies. The
observation schema checks unique IDs, source references and source bounds.
